# Welcome to {brand}

*For everyone.*
Start here. This page tells you what {brand} is for, which pages to read for the
work you do — in order — how the guide is laid out, and where to get help when
you're stuck.

> **{brand} is a map for your data.** It connects to the systems where your data
> already lives and draws a living, interactive picture of how everything is
> connected — so anyone on your team can see where data comes from, where it
> goes, and what depends on it.

---

## What problem does {brand} solve?

Modern data lives in dozens of places: warehouses, pipelines, dashboards,
spreadsheets, models. When something breaks — or when someone asks *"where did
this number come from?"* — the answer is usually trapped in someone's head or
buried in code.

{brand} makes that knowledge **visible and shareable**:

- **See the full picture.** Domains, datasets, tables, columns and dashboards
  appear as **entities** on a canvas, with the connections between them drawn
  as lines. The connections that show data flowing from one thing to another
  are its **lineage**.
- **Trace cause and effect.** Pick any entity and follow its data *upstream*
  (where it came from) or *downstream* (what it feeds) — its *blast radius* —
  before you change anything.
- **Speak a shared language.** A **semantic layer** gives every kind of entity
  one name, colour and icon, so a "Dataset" or a "Domain" means the same thing
  to everyone.
- **Capture and share understanding.** Curate what matters into a **View** and
  share it with your team, so hard-won knowledge doesn't evaporate.

A few principles shape almost every screen:

- **One graph, many lenses.** The data is a single connected graph. Searching,
  tracing, opening a container to see inside it, switching between Business
  and Technical names — each is a way of *looking at* that one graph, never a
  separate copy of it.
- **Looking is safe.** Browsing, searching and tracing never change anything.
  Changing data means deliberately opening a draft, which nobody else sees
  until it's published.
- **Shared context beats personal context.** Saved views and a common semantic
  layer let a team build up a *collective* understanding over time.

New words? [Key Concepts](/guide/key-concepts) explains them in ten minutes, and
the [Glossary & Acronyms](/guide/glossary) has every term in one place.

## Who is this guide for?

Pick the description that sounds most like you and read its pages in order.
Most people are mainly one of these, but you can be all four on different days.
Whatever your path, the [Quick Start](/guide/quick-start) is the best first
ten minutes.

### Viewers — find, read and trace data

You look things up, check where a number came from and follow lineage. Your
path:

1. [Finding Views (the Explorer)](/guide/browsing-views) — find the views your
   team has built.
2. [Reading Lineage](/guide/reading-lineage) — make sense of entities, lines
   and colours.
3. [Tracing Lineage on the Canvas](/guide/exploring-graph) — follow data
   upstream and downstream.
4. [The Lineage Lens & Context View](/guide/lineage-lens) — focus on one
   thing's connections.
5. [Advanced Search](/guide/advanced-search) — find every match in a view and
   act on it.

Hit a locked door on the way? [Requesting Access](/guide/requesting-access)
explains what the message means and how to ask.

### Builders — create and share views

You turn what you've found into views other people use. Your path:

1. [Creating Views](/guide/creating-views) — build a view with **New View**.
2. [Managing & Sharing Views](/guide/managing-views) — decide who sees it, and
   keep your collection tidy.
3. [Display Rules](/guide/display-rules) — tag every entity that matches a
   search with a coloured chip.
4. [The Semantic Layer](/guide/semantic-layer) — shape what your data means.

### Versioning & Change Control — change data safely

You change a graph's data, or review other people's changes. Your path:

1. [Versioning & Change Control](/guide/versioning-change-control) — how drafts,
   reviews and history fit together.
2. [Editing in a Draft](/guide/editing-in-a-draft) — make changes without
   affecting anyone else.
3. [The Review Center](/guide/review-center) — review, approve and merge
   changes.
4. [Import & Export](/guide/import-export) — bulk-load or back up data through
   the same review flow.

### Administrators — connect, govern and run the platform

You connect data, manage people and keep {brand} healthy. Your path:

1. [Admin Setup](/guide/admin-setup) — from a fresh platform to a workspace
   your team can use.
2. [Users & Access](/guide/users-access) — approve people and grant the right
   roles.
3. [Workspace Admin](/guide/workspace-admin) — day-to-day workspace care.
4. [Feature Switches](/guide/feature-switches) — turn capabilities on and off.
5. [The Admin Console](/guide/governance-ops) — health, branding,
   announcements and the audit log.

## Getting help

Help is one click away on every screen of {brand}.

### Open the Help panel

1. Click the question-mark button (**Help**) at the right of the top bar — or
   press `?` anywhere you're not typing.
2. The **Help** panel slides in from the right with the cursor already in its
   search box. Close it with the ✕, by clicking outside it, or with Esc.

The panel has a search box and, below it:

- **Getting started** — your setup checklist ([below](#work-through-the-getting-started-checklist)).
- **Take a tour** — only if your administrator has turned on guided tours. If
  they have, you can replay them from **Help** any time.
- **For this page** — the guide pages about the screen you're on, so the
  answer is usually one click away.
- **Quick start** — a short list of the most-used guide pages.
- **Browse the full user guide** — opens this guide.
- **Contact support** — only if your administrator has set a support email
  (**Administration → Branding → Support email**). It starts an email to that
  address.

### Search the guide and the documentation

1. In the Help panel, type a few words in **Search the docs & user guide…**.
2. Results appear grouped under **Documentation** and **User Guide**, each with
   the passage that matched.
3. Click a **User Guide** result to read it right in the panel — **Back**
   returns to your results, **Open full page** opens it in the guide. A
   **Documentation** result opens in a new tab.

> **Tip:** Every word you type has to appear on the page, so two or three
> specific words find more than a long question.

Reading the guide itself? Use **Search** at the top of the page, or press `⌘K` /
`Ctrl-K` or `/`, to search the same pages.

### Jump anywhere with the command palette

Press `⌘K` (Mac) or `Ctrl-K` (Windows and Linux), or click the search box in the
top bar (**Search pages, views, workspaces, docs…**). The command palette opens.

- **Type to find:** pages and settings, views, workspaces, data sources,
  starter templates, semantic layers, and passages of this guide and the
  documentation (**Docs & Guides**). When many views match, **Show all … in
  Explorer →** lists them all.
- **Before you type**, it offers your **Recent Searches** and **Recent Views**,
  **Switch Workspace** (if you belong to more than one), and quick actions:
  switch between Business and Technical names, **Go to Dashboard**, **Browse
  Views**, **Open the User Guide**, **Toggle Theme** and **Open Settings**.

What it doesn't reach:

- **The data inside a view.** The palette finds views by name, description,
  tag and workspace — not the entities in them. To find an entity, open the
  view and press `/` to search it; see [Advanced Search](/guide/advanced-search).
- **Places you can't open.** Pages and settings your role doesn't allow are
  left out of the results.

### Work through the Getting started checklist

**Getting started** is a checklist of the next useful things to do, tailored to
your role.

1. Click **Getting started** at the bottom of the sidebar (it shows your
   progress, for example **2/6**) — or open **Help** and click **Getting
   started**.
2. The checklist opens in the Help panel, grouped into tracks such as **Explore
   your data**. Which tracks you see depends on your role.
3. Click **Open** on a step. {brand} takes you to the right place, and the
   step's guide page is linked underneath it.

Steps tick themselves off as you work; a few tick off as soon as you click
**Open**. If you joined through an invitation, the checklist opens by itself the
first time you arrive.

Finished, or don't need it? **Hide from sidebar** at the bottom of the checklist
(or the ✕ on the sidebar entry) tucks it away. Bring it back from **Help →
Getting started → Show in sidebar**.

### Tell us whether a page helped

At the end of every guide page, **Was this helpful?** asks for a **Yes** or a
**No**. Choose **No** and you can say what you were looking for, then click
**Send feedback**. When you're signed in, your answer is counted on your
administrators' **Administration → Telemetry** page, which shows which pages
help and which don't.

### Still stuck?

- Check [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting) for common problems and the

- Can't open something? See [Requesting Access](/guide/requesting-access).
- Ask your administrator — or use **Contact support** in the Help panel, if it's
