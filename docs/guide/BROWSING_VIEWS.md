# Browsing Views

*For Viewers.* A **View** is a saved, shareable exploration of your data. Most of
the time, the fastest way to find an answer is to open a View someone has already
built. This page covers finding, opening, and keeping track of them.

> **Tip:** *Why start with Views?* Views are curated. Someone has already chosen
> the right nodes, layout, and filters to tell a clear story. Browsing is the
> low-effort, high-signal way to learn your data landscape.

## The View Gallery

Open **Views** from the sidebar or the Dashboard to reach the gallery — a
browsable grid of every View you have access to.

Each **View card** shows:

- the **name** and **description**,
- the **creator** and **workspace**,
- the **favourite count** (a quick popularity signal), and
- a **visibility badge** — Private, Workspace, Enterprise, or Public. Hover it and it tells you why *you* can see this View.

### Find what you need

| To… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Search by name | Type in the gallery search box — results filter live |
| See what's widely used | Sort by / browse the **Popular** section |
| See what changed recently | Browse the **Recent** section |
| Narrow the field | Filter by **workspace**, **creator**, or **tags** |

> **Tip:** If your team uses consistent tags (e.g. `finance`, `daily-load`),
> filtering by tag is the quickest way to the right View. See
> [Ways of Working](/guide/ways-of-working) for tagging conventions.

Large galleries don't dump everything at once — the grid shows a first page and
a **Load more** control brings in the next batch, so browsing stays fast even
across hundreds of Views.

## Opening a View

Click any card to open the View on the **canvas**. It loads exactly as it was
saved — the same visible nodes, layout, layers, granularity, and filters.

From here you can:

- **Pan and zoom** to move around the picture (drag to pan, scroll to zoom).
- **Click any node** to open its details panel.
- **Trace further** if you want to explore beyond the saved snapshot — your
  changes are temporary and don't alter the saved View unless you deliberately
  update it.

Learn to interpret what you see in [Reading Lineage](/guide/reading-lineage).

> **Note:** Looking never breaks anything. Exploring inside a View is completely
> safe. To change the saved View itself, you must be its owner (or have edit
> access) and explicitly save.

## Visibility — who can see a View

| Visibility | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | You, anyone you share it with, and your workspace's admins |
| **Workspace** | Everyone with access to that workspace |
| **Enterprise** | Everyone in the organisation, including people outside that workspace |
| **Public** | Everyone signed in to the platform |

In addition, a View's owner can **explicitly share** it with specific people or
groups as an *editor* or *viewer*, even across the boundaries above. If you
can't find a View someone mentioned, you may simply not have been granted
access — ask them to share it. See [Managing Views](/guide/managing-views).

## Favourites and quick access

When you open a View you'll use often, click the **★ favourite** icon. Favourited
Views appear in the **Quick Access** section of the left sidebar, so you can jump
back with a single click from anywhere in the app.

Favourites are personal — yours won't clutter anyone else's sidebar.

---

## Switching context

Remember that Views belong to a **workspace**. If a View looks empty or you can't
find one you expect, open **Workspaces** from the sidebar to see the ones you have
access to, then enter the one you want to work in — each screen shows you which
workspace you're in, there's no separate global switcher to keep in sync. Entering
a different workspace changes which Views — and which data — are available.
